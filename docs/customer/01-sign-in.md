## Who can do this

Anybody with a Tanzanian mobile number. You do not need an account first —
entering a number Otesha has never seen creates one, and takes you through
[[link:customer/welcome|two short questions]] before you land.

## Before you start

Your phone, with signal. Nothing else.

## Steps

**1. Type your phone number and tap Send code.**

![The Otesha sign-in screen. Marked on the screenshot: Your phone number, starting 07. Sends a six-digit code by SMS.](/img/customer-sign-in-01-en.png "The sign-in screen at otesha.co.tz — the first thing you see.")

Type it the way you say it, starting `07`. Otesha adds the `+255` for you.

**2. Enter the six digits and tap Continue.**

![The code screen. Marked on the screenshot: The six digits from the SMS. Signs you in.](/img/customer-sign-in-02-en.png "The code arrives by SMS, usually within a few seconds.")

The code lasts five minutes and the screen counts down, so you can see how long
is left. Under it, **Send another code** is greyed out for a short while — the
countdown on the button is telling you when you may ask again, not that
something has gone wrong.

## How you know it worked

![My trees, the screen you land on after signing in. Marked on the screenshot: My trees — where signing in lands you. Plant your first tree, if you have none yet.](/img/customer-sign-in-03-en.png "Signing in lands you on My trees.")

You land on **My trees**. If you have not sponsored a tree yet, the page says so
and offers **Plant your first tree** — that is the right screen, not an error.

The first time you sign in you will be asked your name first. That is
[[link:customer/welcome|Karibu Otesha]], and it takes a moment.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** *"Too many verification codes requested. Try again later."* — Otesha
> allows five codes an hour for one number. Wait fifteen minutes and try again.
> Asking for more codes during that window does not shorten it.

> **Warning:** The screen says a code was sent but nothing arrives. Check the
> number shown above the code box is yours — it is displayed there for exactly
> this reason. If it is wrong, tap **Use a different number**.
