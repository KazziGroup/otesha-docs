## Who can do this

Anybody signing in for the first time. You will not see this screen again —
entering a number Otesha has never seen creates an account, and this is the step
between the code and your trees.

## Before you start

Nothing. It asks for a name and, if you want, an email address.

## Steps

**1. Type your name.**

![Karibu Otesha, the first-time setup screen, with the name and email fields marked. Marked on the screenshot: Your name. This is required. Optional — where receipts go. You can add it later.](/img/customer-welcome-01-en.png "Karibu Otesha — two things and you are in.")

This is what Otesha calls you in the app and on your receipts. It is the one
thing on this screen that is required, marked with a red asterisk.

**2. Add an email address, or skip it.**

Email is optional and labelled so. It is where receipts and updates about your
trees go — so if you want a record of what you have sponsored somewhere other
than the app, put one in.

It can also become a second way to sign in. The screen says so: *"To sign in
with it too, verify it later in Your account."* Adding it here does not verify
it; that is a separate step you take when you want it.

**3. Tap Continue — or Skip for now.**

**Skip for now** takes you straight to your trees without an email. It does not
skip your name.

## How you know it worked

You land on **My trees**, the same screen you will see every time you sign in
from now on.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** You skipped the email and want one after all. Nothing is lost — you
> can add it later from your profile, which is what "You can add it later" on
> this screen is promising.

> **Warning:** Continue does nothing. The name field is required; if it is empty
> the form will not submit. Check for a message under the field.
