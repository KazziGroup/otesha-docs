## Who can do this

Anybody signed in.

## Before you start

[[link:customer/browse-species|Choose a species and a care plan]]. The cart holds
plans, not bare species — a tree and the looking-after that goes with it.

## Steps

**1. Add a tree, then open your cart.**

![The cart in the customer app, with the server-total note and the check out control marked. Marked on the screenshot: The total is worked out server-side at checkout. Goes to a confirmation; nothing is charged here.](/img/customer-cart-01-en.png "Your cart. One line per care plan, with a quantity.")

Each line is a care plan with a quantity. Change the number to sponsor several
of the same tree; the bin removes the line.

**2. Read the note under the subtotal.**

*"The total is calculated by our server when you check out."* The subtotal here
is arithmetic done in your phone; the amount you are actually asked for is
worked out by Otesha. They should agree — but the second one is the one that
counts.

**3. Check out when the cart is right.**

## How you know it worked

The cart shows what you expect and **Check out** takes you to a confirmation.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** Nothing in the cart is reserved. A species
> [[link:admin/species-record|withdrawn from sale]] or a
> [[link:admin/publish-price|repriced plan]] can change what you see between
> visits.
