## Who can do this

Anybody signed in with something in their [[link:customer/cart|cart]].

## Before you start

The phone you want charged, with signal. It does not have to be the number you
signed in with.

## Steps

**1. Confirm the order.**

![The confirmation step in the customer app, with the reassurance and the place-order control marked. Marked on the screenshot: Said twice on this screen, and true. Creates the order, then asks how to pay.](/img/customer-checkout-01-en.png "Confirm your order. Nothing has been charged yet.")

The screen says **Nothing has been charged yet** twice, above and below the
total. **Place order** creates the order — it does not take any money.

**2. Choose how to pay, and which number to charge.**

![The payment step in the customer app, with the payment methods and the number-to-charge note marked. Marked on the screenshot: Six ways to pay, including card and bank transfer. The number charged need not be the one you signed in with.](/img/customer-checkout-02-en.png "How would you like to pay. Six methods, and the number to prompt.")

Six methods: M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, Halo Pesa, card and bank transfer.
The **number to charge** is separate from your account: *"You can pay from a
different number — a parent's, or your own second line."*

**3. Send the prompt, then approve it on the phone.**

## How you know it worked

The page waits and tells you so — *"This page will update on its own — you do
not need to refresh."* When the payment settles, the order stops saying
**Not paid**.

## If it goes wrong

> **Warning:** Closing the page does not cancel anything. The order is waiting
> in [[link:customer/orders|Your orders]] with **Pay for this order** on it.

> **Note:** The prompt goes to the number you typed, not the one you signed in
> with. If nothing arrives, check that number first.
