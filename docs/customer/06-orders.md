## Who can do this

Anybody signed in.

## Before you start

Nothing.

## Steps

**1. Open Orders.**

![Your orders in the customer app, with the unpaid explanation and the pay control marked. Marked on the screenshot: An unpaid order is held, not lost. Picks the payment up where you left it.](/img/customer-orders-01-en.png "Your orders. Each one carries a reference and where it has got to.")

Each order carries a reference like `ORD-A20546AC`, what you ordered, when it
was placed, and its state.

**2. Read what an unpaid order means.**

*"Nothing has been charged. This order is held for you, and no tree is planted
until it is paid."* Both halves matter: no money has moved, and no tree exists
yet either.

**3. Pay for it, if that is what is missing.**

## How you know it worked

The order stops saying **Not paid**, and the tree appears under
[[link:customer/tree-detail|My trees]] once Otesha has planted it.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** Paid but no tree yet is normal. Planting happens on the ground, on a
> planting date the operation sets — see
> [[link:admin/fulfilment|how orders are fulfilled]].

> **Warning:** Do not place a second order because the first looks stuck. Pay
> the one that is already there; two orders are two trees and two charges.
