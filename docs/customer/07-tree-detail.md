## Who can do this

Anybody who has sponsored a tree.

## Before you start

A tree of your own — which is exactly what is missing here.

## Steps

> **Note:** **This page has no screenshots, on purpose.**
>
> Every tree in the environment these manuals are captured from belongs to a
> corporate account, not to a customer. Getting a customer a tree means
> completing a sponsorship, and the payment provider used here only settles
> through a signed webhook, so a capture cannot get past checkout.
>
> The screen exists and works. What is missing is a customer who owns something
> to look at, which is a matter of what the database was seeded with rather than
> anything about the app.
>
> The nearest thing we can show you is the same tree from the other side:
> [[link:customer/orders|your orders]] tracks a sponsorship up to the point the
> tree is planted.

## How you know it worked

The tree shows its species, where it stands, its state, and the photographs a
caretaker has added from the field.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** A tree with no photographs is not a neglected tree. Photographs are
> optional when a caretaker records work, so their absence says nothing about
> how the tree is doing.
