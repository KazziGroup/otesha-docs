## Who can do this

Anybody signed in. You do not need to have sponsored anything before.

## Before you start

Nothing. It is worth knowing roughly what you want the tree *for* — shade,
fruit, carbon — because that is how the list is organised.

## Steps

**1. Tap Plant a tree.**

![The species list in the customer app, with the category filter and a care plan count marked. Marked on the screenshot: Filter by what the tree is for. How many ways this species can be looked after.](/img/customer-browse-species-01-en.png "Plant a tree. Eight species, filtered by what they are for.")

Every species shows its carbon a year, how long it takes to reach maturity, and
the price it starts from. The categories — indigenous, fruit, shade,
agroforestry, mangrove — are the quickest way to narrow it.

**2. Open one to see what looking after it involves.**

![A species page in the customer app, with the indicative carbon note and Add to cart marked. Marked on the screenshot: The carbon figure is an estimate, and says so. Starts a sponsorship at this plan's price.](/img/customer-browse-species-02-en.png "A species, its figures, and the care plan on offer.")

A **care plan** is the part worth reading twice: it is what Otesha does for the
tree and for how long — *36 months, watering every 3 days, full care* — with a
monthly price. Some species offer more than one.

**3. Add it to your cart.**

## How you know it worked

The tree is in your cart at that plan's price, ready to
[[link:customer/checkout|pay for]].

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** The carbon figure is marked *±10% · indicative* and means it.
> Survival rate is history — what has happened to trees of this species — not a
> guarantee about yours.

> **Warning:** A species you saw before may no longer be listed. Otesha
> sometimes withdraws one from sale; trees already in the ground are unaffected,
> including any you already sponsor.
