## Who can do this

Any signed-in caretaker.

## Before you start

Nothing.

## Steps

**1. Tap the earnings card on Today, or open it from your profile.**

![The earnings screen in the caretaker app, with the payment method note marked. Marked on the screenshot: How and how often the money arrives.](/img/caretaker-earnings-01-en.png "Earnings. Empty until a payout run has been paid out.")

**2. Read what it says about timing.**

*"Paid monthly via M-Pesa."* Money arrives on a cycle, not per task.

## How you know it worked

Amounts appear once a [[link:admin/release-payout|payout run]] covering your
work has been compiled, approved and disbursed. Until then the screen is empty
even if you have logged plenty.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** **Logged work is not the same as paid work.** The screen can read
> empty while your profile shows dozens of logs this month — that means the work
> is recorded and a run has not yet paid it out, which is normal between cycles.

> **Warning:** Work that was [[link:admin/flagged-work|flagged]] is not in any
> run. If the app has told you *"1 log was not accepted. Show a supervisor."*,
> that work stays unpaid until somebody at the office decides on it.

> **Note:** Money can also be [[link:admin/payment-holds|held]] deliberately,
> against you or against your whole zone. A hold has a reason recorded with it,
> and your supervisor can tell you what it says.
