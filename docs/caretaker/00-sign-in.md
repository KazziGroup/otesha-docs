## Who can do this

Caretakers Otesha has already enrolled. The screen says so plainly: *"Only a
number Otesha has already enrolled can sign in. If yours does not work, your
supervisor registers it."*

This is the one way Otesha works differently from the customer app. There,
typing a new number creates an account. Here it cannot — a caretaker is somebody
an administrator [[link:admin/approve-caretaker|approved]] and put on a section,
so the account exists before you ever open the app.

## Before you start

The phone with the number your supervisor registered, and enough signal to
receive one SMS. Everything after signing in works offline; this step does not.

## Steps

**1. Type your phone number and tap Send me a code.**

![The caretaker app's sign-in screen, with the phone field and the Send me a code button marked. Marked on the screenshot: The number your zone lead registered for you. Sends a six-digit code by SMS.](/img/caretaker-sign-in-01-en.png "The caretaker app opens here until you have signed in once.")

**2. Enter the six digits from the SMS.**

## How you know it worked

The app opens on **Today**, greeting you by name — *Habari, Salma* — with the
day's trees on the card beneath. You will not be asked to sign in again on that
phone unless you sign out.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** The code does not arrive. Check you typed the registered number and
> not a second phone you also use. If it is right and nothing comes, your
> supervisor can confirm which number is on your record.

> **Warning:** *"Only a number Otesha has already enrolled can sign in."* — this
> is not a typo in your number, it is an account that does not exist yet.
> Nothing you can do on this screen will fix it; somebody has to register you.
