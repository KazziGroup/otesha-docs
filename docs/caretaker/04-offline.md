## Who can do this

Any signed-in caretaker. Signing in is the one thing that needs signal; the work
does not.

## Before you start

Sign in while you have signal. After that the app carries what it needs.

## Steps

**1. Work normally.**

![The watering form in the caretaker app, with the GPS lock, the offline note and the method marked. Marked on the screenshot: The handset has a fix, and how good it is. Position and time are recorded for you, with or without signal. How the water was carried.](/img/caretaker-log-watering-01-en.png "The promise is on the form itself: works offline, syncs later.")

The form says it plainly: *"GPS + time recorded automatically · works offline,
syncs later."* Position comes from the handset, not the network, so a log made
with no bars carries the same evidence as one made with full signal.

**2. Keep logging.**

Logs wait on the phone. Nothing is lost by carrying on.

**3. Come back into range and open the app.**

## How you know it worked

The counts on [[link:caretaker/today|Today]] and
[[link:caretaker/profile|your profile]] move when the logs reach the server —
which is when you are back in range, not when you tapped save. A count that has
not moved yet is a queue, not a loss.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** Some things genuinely need signal and say so. A tree's recent
> activity is one: *"Recent activity loads when you have signal."* The tree
> itself, your section and the day's tasks were downloaded earlier and are all
> there.

> **Warning:** A log that arrives without a usable position is not simply
> accepted late — it is [[link:admin/flagged-work|flagged]], and flagged work
> cannot be paid until somebody decides on it. If the app says *"1 log was not
> accepted. Show a supervisor."*, do that rather than logging the work again.
