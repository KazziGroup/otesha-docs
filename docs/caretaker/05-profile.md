## Who can do this

Any signed-in caretaker. This is your own record — nobody else's.

## Before you start

Nothing. It works from whatever the app last downloaded, so it opens with or
without signal.

## Steps

**1. Tap Profile.**

**2. Read the line under your name.**

It says your zone and your status — *Zone 87 · Active*. **Active** is what lets
you be given trees and be paid; if it says anything else, that is the thing to
ask your supervisor about before anything else on this screen.

**3. Check the three counts.**

**Your trees** is how many are in your care. **Logs this month** is the work you
have recorded. **Days worked** is how many days you recorded anything. Below
them, **This month so far** is money —
[[link:caretaker/earnings|what you have earned]] so far this month.

## How you know it worked

The counts move as you work. Logs this month goes up when a log reaches the
server, which for work recorded [[link:caretaker/offline|with no signal]] is
when you are next in range rather than when you tapped save.
