## Who can do this

Any signed-in caretaker, for any tree in their section.

## Before you start

Nothing. Tap a task on [[link:caretaker/today|Today]], or find the tree in
[[link:caretaker/trees|your section]].

## Steps

**1. Tap the tree.**

![A tree in the caretaker app, with its cluster and the log control marked. Marked on the screenshot: Where the tree is, to the metre. Records work against this tree.](/img/caretaker-tree-01-en.png "One tree: its species, age, and where it stands.")

The card names it three ways — the code, the species in English and Kiswahili,
and the scientific name — with how far along it is.

**2. Read the three figures.**

**Age**, **Watered** and **CO₂ / year**. A dash under *Watered* means no
watering has been logged against this tree, which for a newly assigned section
is normal rather than alarming.

**3. Check where it is.**

The cluster and the coordinates are what the app matches your position against
when you log work. That match is why a log can be
[[link:admin/flagged-work|flagged]].

## How you know it worked

**Log activity** at the bottom takes you to the form for whatever the tree is
due.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** *"Recent activity loads when you have signal."* The tree's history
> lives on the server; everything else on this screen was downloaded earlier and
> works without it.
