## Who can do this

Any approved caretaker with a section. If your app lists no trees, you have not
been given one yet — that is an office problem, not a phone problem.

## Before you start

Nothing. Today works without signal; anything you log queues on the phone and
goes up when you are back in range.

## Steps

**1. Open the app.**

![The Today screen in the caretaker app, with the task card and the earnings card marked. Marked on the screenshot: What needs doing today, and how much is done. What you have earned this month.](/img/caretaker-today-01-en.png "Today. The task card counts the trees, not the visits.")

**Today** is where the app opens. The top card is the day's work — how many
trees are done out of how many, and what kind of work it is. The count is trees,
not visits: a tree needing both a watering and a check counts once.

**2. Check what needs attention.**

Below the day's work sits anything flagged, named by tree so you can find it.

## How you know it worked

The task card's count goes up as you log work, and the bar fills. **This month
so far** is what you have earned, and it updates as work is accepted.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** The count does not move after you log something. If you are out of
> signal the work is queued on the phone, not lost. It goes up when you are back
> in range, and the count moves then.
