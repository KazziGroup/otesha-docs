## Who can do this

Nobody, yet — and that is the point of this page.

## Before you start

If a colleague has shown you streaks, challenges, a certification ladder or a
shareable poster on their phone, they were running a demo build. This page
explains why you cannot find those screens, and why the manual does not document
them.

## Steps

> **Note:** **None of it is real yet.**
>
> The caretaker app fences every screen that is designed but not built behind a
> single marker. A screen behind it renders in a demo build and nowhere else, so
> a caretaker on a real build is never shown a figure Otesha cannot compute.
>
> Documenting them would describe a feature that does not exist — a level nobody
> can reach, badges nobody can earn, a streak nothing counts.

**What is fenced today:** streaks, challenges, milestones, Species IQ, the
wishlist, the shareable poster, the certification ladder and badge row on your
profile, the earn-more screen, the QR code, and the drone and tree-preview
screens.

**What is real, on the same screens:** your zone and status, the trees in your
care, logs this month, days worked, what you have earned, and the activity strip
on your profile — its streak is counted from logs that actually exist.

## How you know it worked

Nothing to do. If a screen is not in your app, it is not missing — it has not
been built.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** For staff: the marker is `StaticOnly`, and grepping for it in the
> caretaker app gives the current inventory of invented product. That is
> deliberate — *"what is still invented" should be a question the codebase
> answers in one command*. When a mechanic gets a table, a route and a real
> number behind it, the wrapper comes off and this page gets shorter.

> **Note:** Two of this manual's figures — [[link:caretaker/trees|your section]]
> and [[link:caretaker/profile|your profile]] — are shot against the demo build,
> because the dev client needs a packager it cannot always reach. Their crops
> stop above the fenced parts, so nothing invented appears in them.
