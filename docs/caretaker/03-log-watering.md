## Who can do this

Any signed-in caretaker, standing at the tree.

## Before you start

Be at the tree. The app records where you are, and that is what ties the work to
the right tree.

## Steps

**1. Open the tree and tap Log activity.**

![The watering form in the caretaker app, with the GPS lock, the offline note and the method marked. Marked on the screenshot: The handset has a fix, and how good it is. Position and time are recorded for you, with or without signal. How the water was carried.](/img/caretaker-log-watering-01-en.png "Logging a watering. The tree, the amount, and how it was carried.")

**2. Check the fix before you fill anything in.**

The tree card shows **GPS locked** and how accurate it is — *5 m*. That figure
is the difference between a log that counts and one that gets
[[link:admin/flagged-work|flagged]] and held.

**3. Set the amount and the method.**

The form starts at the default and tells you what it is: *20 L for young trees
under twelve months*. **Method** is how the water was carried — bucket, drip or
bowser.

**4. Add a photo if there is something to show, then save.**

## How you know it worked

The form closes and the day's count on [[link:caretaker/today|Today]] goes up.

## If it goes wrong

> **Warning:** *"1 log was not accepted. Show a supervisor."* A log that could
> not be placed does not just disappear — it goes to the office as
> [[link:admin/flagged-work|flagged work]], and **it cannot be paid until
> somebody decides on it**. Show a supervisor rather than logging it again.

> **Note:** *"GPS + time recorded automatically · works offline, syncs later."*
> You never type either. With no signal the log waits on the phone and goes up
> when you are next in range — see
> [[link:caretaker/offline|working with no signal]].
