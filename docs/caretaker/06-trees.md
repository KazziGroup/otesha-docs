## Who can do this

Any signed-in caretaker who has been
[[link:admin/assign-cluster|given a section]]. If the office has not done that
yet, this screen is empty however hard you look.

## Before you start

Nothing. It lists what the app last downloaded, so it opens without signal.

## Steps

**1. Tap Trees.**

Under the heading is your zone and how many trees are in your care. Each row is
one tree: its code, how old it is, when it was last watered, and how far along
it is — **Growing**, **Mature**, and so on.

**2. Filter to what needs doing.**

The pills across the top narrow the list. **Needs attention** is the one to
start a day with; **Growing**, **Mature** and **Gone** answer different
questions.

**3. Open a tree to see its history.**

## How you know it worked

The count under the heading matches what the office thinks you have. If it does
not, that is a rota question rather than an app one.
