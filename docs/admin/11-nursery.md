## Who can do this

Anybody with access to the console. Sowing and planting need operations staff.

## Before you start

Nothing to read the list.

## Steps

**1. Open Nursery.**

![The nursery list in the admin console, with the tree IDs column and the waiting-on-tags count marked. Marked on the screenshot: The tree IDs this batch reserved. Batches whose tags have not been printed.](/img/admin-nursery-01-en.png "Nursery. One row per batch, from sowing to tagging.")

A batch is seedlings sown together at one nursery for one zone — `BAT-KIL0203`,
167 seedlings, sown 14 August.

**2. Read the tree IDs.**

A batch reserves a **range** of tree IDs when it is sown:
`KIL-02-CP-001-0110 → KIL-02-CP-004-0148`. Those identifiers exist before the
trees are in the ground, which is what lets a tag be printed and carried to
site.

**3. Check what is waiting on tags.**

The count at the top is batches whose tags have not been printed. A batch cannot
be planted against its identifiers until they have.

## How you know it worked

The **Tags** column reads *printed* with a date, and the waiting count drops.
**Reprint** is there for tags that were lost or damaged.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** Seedlings sown is not trees planted. A batch stays here until the
> trees go in the ground, and survival is measured from planting rather than
> from sowing.
