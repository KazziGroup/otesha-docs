## Who can do this

Staff who manage zones.

## Before you start

Nothing — but read the note below before you look for this screen in the manual.

## Steps

> **Note:** **This page has no screenshots, on purpose.**
>
> Tracing a boundary is drawing on a map, and the environment these manuals are
> captured from has no Mapbox token. The screen renders an empty panel where the
> map should be. A figure of that would document a configuration problem rather
> than the task, and a reader matching it against their own screen would
> conclude the manual was describing something broken.
>
> What we can tell you without a picture: a boundary drawn rather than walked is
> recorded as **provisional** and marked with an asterisk wherever its hectares
> appear. See [[link:admin/zones|the zone list]], which shows that marking and
> warns that provisional hectares overstate the real site.

## How you know it worked

The zone's **surveyed** date changes, and the asterisk against its hectares goes
away.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** A provisional area that reaches an ESG figure overstates what Otesha
> manages. Walk the perimeter before the number is quoted to anybody.
