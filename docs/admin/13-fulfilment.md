## Who can do this

Operations staff and above.

## Before you start

Know which site the trees are going to. That is the decision this page is
waiting on.

## Steps

**1. Open Fulfilment.**

![The fulfilment queue in the admin console, with an unassigned planting site and the lateness marked. Marked on the screenshot: Paid for, but nobody has said where it goes. How long past its planting date the order is.](/img/admin-fulfilment-01-en.png "Fulfilment. Paid orders, what they bought, and when planting was due.")

Every row is an order somebody has paid for. **Bought** is what they bought —
`10× Baobab` — and **Planting due** is when it was promised.

**2. Look at the planting site.**

**No site chosen** means nobody has said where these trees go. Until that is
decided the order cannot be fulfilled, and the customer sees a paid order with
no tree.

**3. Fulfil the order.**

## How you know it worked

The row leaves the queue, and the trees appear to the customer under My trees
with identifiers from [[link:admin/nursery|a nursery batch]].

## If it goes wrong

> **Warning:** *"87 days late"* is counted from the planting date the customer
> was given, not from when you opened this page. Lateness here is a promise
> somebody has already been given.

> **Note:** Trees cannot be planted against identifiers whose tags have not been
> printed. If a batch is holding the queue up, check
> [[link:admin/nursery|the nursery]].
