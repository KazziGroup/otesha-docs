## Who can do this

Operations staff and above. Anybody filed for review by
[[link:admin/new-caretaker|registering them without approving]] waits here until
somebody decides.

## Before you start

Nothing. The queue is a tab on the Caretakers page and the number beside it is
how many are waiting.

## Steps

**1. Open Caretakers and switch to Review queue.**

![The review queue in the admin console, with the identity document note and the Approve button marked. Marked on the screenshot: Whether an ID scan is on file. It is not shown, only noted. Approving records you as the approver.](/img/admin-approve-caretaker-01-en.png "Review queue. Each card is one person waiting, with when they registered.")

Each card is one person: their name, the number they will sign in with, their
zone, and when they registered. Some have been waiting months — the dates are
worth reading.

**2. Check what the card tells you about identity.**

A card says either that an ID scan is on file or that none was uploaded. It does
not show you the scan; nothing does. That is deliberate — the file goes to a
restricted bucket and the record only ever notes its existence.

**3. Approve or reject.**

**Approving records you as the approver.** That is the whole reason this queue
exists rather than everyone being enrolled on sight.

## How you know it worked

The card leaves the queue, the count beside the tab drops by one, and their
status in the directory reads **Active**.

## If it goes wrong

> **Warning:** Approving somebody is not the end of it. A caretaker with no
> section is the amber banner at the top of this page: their app lists no trees,
> the 06:00 compile gives them no tasks, and the evening sweep then holds their
> pay for not logging work nobody assigned. Set the rota in the same sitting.

> **Note:** No identity document is not a reason to reject on its own — most of
> this queue has none. It is one thing to weigh, not a gate.
