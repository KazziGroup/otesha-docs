## Who can do this

Anybody with access to the console.

## Before you start

Nothing.

## Steps

**1. Open Species & pricing.**

![The species catalogue in the admin console, with the on-sale filter and the catalogue column marked. Marked on the screenshot: Narrow to what customers can actually buy. Whether it is offered for sale today.](/img/admin-species-01-en.png "Species & pricing. One row per species, with its cheapest plan and its catalogue status.")

Each species carries three names — English, Kiswahili and scientific — and sits
in one category: indigenous, fruit, shade, agroforestry or mangrove.

**2. Read across the row.**

**From** is the cheapest care plan. **Plans** is how many there are. **CO₂/yr**
is an estimate and is marked as one wherever it appears. **Survival** is what
has actually happened to trees of this species.

**3. Use On sale only to see the customer's view.**

**Catalogue** says whether a species is offered today. A species can be in the
list, fully described, and not for sale — see
[[link:admin/species-record|the species record]] for what withdrawing does and
does not change.

## How you know it worked

Nothing to confirm; this is a list.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** Survival is history, not a promise. A species with a low rate has
> done badly somewhere — that may be the species, or it may be the site.
