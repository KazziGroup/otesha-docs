## Who can do this

Operations staff and above.

## Before you start

Be clear which of the three names you are changing. Two of them are editable and
one is not, and the console says so on the form.

## Steps

**1. Open Species & pricing and choose a species.**

![A species record in the admin console, with the withdraw control and the fixed-name note marked. Marked on the screenshot: A catalogue decision. Trees already in the ground are unaffected. The three names are fixed once trees point at them.](/img/admin-species-record-01-en.png "A species record, open on Details.")

**2. Change what the species is.**

Category, water needs, carbon per year, years to maturity and expected survival
are all editable, and the carbon figure is labelled *±10% · indicative* wherever
it is shown.

**3. Leave the names alone unless you mean it.**

The form is explicit: *"Fixed. Every tree planted as this species points at it —
a correction is a new species and a decision about those trees."* Renaming is
not a typo fix; it changes what thousands of planted trees claim to be.

**4. Withdraw from sale, if that is what you mean.**

*"Withdrawing stops it being offered. Trees already in the ground are unaffected
— this is a catalogue decision, not a botanical one."*

## How you know it worked

**Catalogue** in the list reads differently, and the species stops appearing to
customers [[link:customer/browse-species|choosing a tree]].

## If it goes wrong

> **Warning:** Withdrawing does not cancel anything. Existing sponsorships,
> subscriptions and caretaker rounds carry on exactly as before.

> **Note:** A misspelt scientific name is worth raising rather than quietly
> fixing. The correction is a new species record and a decision about every tree
> already pointing at the old one.
