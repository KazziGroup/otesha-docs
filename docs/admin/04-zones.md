## Who can do this

Anybody with access to the console.

## Before you start

Nothing.

## Steps

**1. Open Zones.**

![The zone list in the admin console, with the provisional boundary warning and the Surveyed column marked. Marked on the screenshot: Provisional boundaries overstate their hectares. When somebody last walked the perimeter.](/img/admin-zones-01-en.png "Zones. One row per site, with its area, trees and survival rate.")

A zone is a site: a school, a woodlot, a strip of open land. Each row carries a
code like `DOD-03`, the region, the site type, its hectares, how many trees are
alive out of how many were planted, and the survival rate that follows from
those two numbers.

**2. Read the banner before you trust the hectares.**

Some boundaries are **provisional** — drawn rather than walked. They are dashed
on the map, marked with an asterisk in the table, and shown in amber. The banner
says what that costs: *their hectares overstate the real site*.

**3. Check when the site was last surveyed.**

The **Surveyed** column is when somebody last walked the perimeter. An old date
next to a provisional boundary is the combination worth chasing.

## How you know it worked

Nothing to confirm — this page is a list. Filters narrow it by region and by
status.

## If it goes wrong

> **Warning:** A provisional area that reaches an ESG or SDG figure overstates
> what Otesha actually manages. Walk the perimeter before those hectares are
> published — see [[link:corporate/esg-report|Download an ESG report]] for where
> they end up.

> **Note:** The map panel needs a Mapbox token. Without one the console says so
> and the table still works; the list is not affected.
