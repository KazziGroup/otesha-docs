## Who can do this

Anybody who can sign in to the console, for their own account only.

## Before you start

Your **current password**, if you are changing it.

## Steps

**1. Open Settings, then Your account.**

![The account settings page in the admin console, with the password reason and the phone note marked. Marked on the screenshot: Your current password is asked for even though you are signed in. Your phone links your records, so it is not yours to edit.](/img/admin-account-01-en.png "Your account: your details, your password, and what this session may do.")

**2. Edit your name, email or language, and save.**

Your **email** is what you sign in with.

**3. Change your password.**

Enter the current one, then a new one of **at least 12 characters**.

## How you know it worked

The page confirms the change. A new password applies to your next sign-in.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** You are asked for your current password even though you are already
> signed in. The reason is an unattended laptop, not distrust.

> **Note:** Your **phone number** is changed by an administrator, because it
> links your partner records. Ask somebody with user administration —
> see [[link:admin/users|the users list]].
