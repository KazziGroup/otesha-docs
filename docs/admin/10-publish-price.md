## Who can do this

Operations staff and above.

## Before you start

Know which plan you are repricing. A species can carry several.

## Steps

**1. Open the species and switch to Care plans & pricing.**

![A species care plan in the admin console, with the in-force date and the change-price control marked. Marked on the screenshot: Prices carry the date they came into force. A new price; the old one stays on orders already placed.](/img/admin-publish-price-01-en.png "Care plans & pricing. One card per plan, with its price and when it started.")

A care plan is what Otesha actually does for the tree — *36 months · watering
every 3 days · full* — with a monthly price attached.

**2. Read the in-force date before changing anything.**

Every price carries the date it started. That date is the point: prices are a
history, not a single number.

**3. Change the price, retire the plan, or add another.**

**Change price** starts a new price from today. **Retire** stops the plan being
offered. **Add a care plan** gives the species a second way to be looked after —
which is what makes **Plans** in the catalogue read more than one.

## How you know it worked

The card shows the new price with today's date, and
[[link:customer/browse-species|the customer's view]] shows it on the species.

## If it goes wrong

> **Warning:** A new price does not reprice anything already sold. Sponsorships
> and subscriptions keep the price in force when they were taken out, which is
> why the date is on the card.

> **Note:** Retiring the last plan on a species leaves it describable but
> unbuyable. If that is what you want,
> [[link:admin/species-record|withdrawing it from sale]] says so more clearly.
