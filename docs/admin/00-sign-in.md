## Who can do this

Otesha staff. The console says **Staff accounts only** under the heading, and it
means it — a sponsor's or a caretaker's account cannot sign in here, however
valid it is elsewhere.

Staff accounts are made for you by an administrator. Unlike every other Otesha
app, typing an address the system has never seen does not create anything.

## Before you start

Your work email address and your password.

## Steps

**1. Enter your email and password, then Sign in.**

![The admin console sign-in screen, with the email field and the Sign in button marked. Marked on the screenshot: Your work email address. No code is sent — this door uses a password.](/img/admin-sign-in-01-en.png "The console at its sign-in screen. Staff accounts only.")

No code is sent. This is the only Otesha front door with a password, which is
worth knowing if you also use the customer app or the corporate portal and are
waiting for an SMS that will not come.

## How you know it worked

You land on the **Dashboard**, with the navigation down the left — Caretakers,
Rota, Flagged work, Zones and the rest. What you can see there depends on your
role.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** Your password works elsewhere but not here. Check you are on the
> console and not the corporate portal — they look similar and sign in
> completely differently.

> **Warning:** Repeated failures lock the account rather than simply refusing.
> If you are unsure of the password, ask for a reset rather than guessing.
