## Who can do this

Operations staff and above. These decisions release money, so they follow the
[[link:admin/permissions|permissions matrix]].

## Before you start

Nothing. The queue is a tab count on the page.

## Steps

**1. Open Flagged work.**

![The flagged work queue in the admin console, with the unpaid warning and the reason column marked. Marked on the screenshot: Flagged work is unpaid work until it is decided. Why the log could not be placed.](/img/admin-flagged-work-01-en.png "Flagged work. Each row is work somebody did that the system could not place.")

The page states the stake in its first line: *"Work that was recorded but could
not be placed. None of it can be paid until somebody decides."*

**2. Read why each one was flagged.**

The reasons are about the handset, not the person — *"the handset never got a
fix"*, *"the position could not be checked"*. A caretaker under tree cover or
inside a building can do the work perfectly and still land here.

**3. Decide.**

## How you know it worked

The row moves from **Waiting** to **Decided**, and the work becomes payable in
the next [[link:admin/release-payout|payout run]].

## If it goes wrong

> **Warning:** Leaving the queue alone is a decision with a cost. Every row is
> somebody who did the work and has not been paid for it, and the caretaker's
> app is telling them to show a supervisor.

> **Note:** Several rows from the same caretaker on the same day usually mean a
> place rather than a person — a site where the handset cannot get a fix.
