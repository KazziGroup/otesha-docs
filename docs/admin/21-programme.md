## Who can do this

Staff with settings permissions.

## Before you start

Know that these are **organisation-wide**. A change here reaches printed
materials, partner-facing documents and every zone created afterwards.

## Steps

**1. Open Settings, then Programme.**

![The programme settings page in the admin console, with the audit note and an unset default marked. Marked on the screenshot: The audit trail is the setting's own history, not a separate screen. A default in use says so, rather than looking like a choice somebody made.](/img/admin-programme-01-en.png "Programme settings: identity at the top, field operations below.")

**2. Change what you need.**

**Organisation** is shown on printed QR sheets and partner documents. **Contact
email** is where a partner or customer is told to write. **Support phone** is
printed on caretaker materials.

**3. Check the field operations defaults.**

**Default season for new zones** is a starting value, not a switch — season is
per zone, because a woodlot in Dodoma is in a different season from one in
Moshi. **Planting promise** is the number of days the fulfilment page measures
lateness against.

## How you know it worked

The field shows **Last changed**, with the date and time.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** *"Every change is recorded with who made it and what it was
> before."* That is the audit trail — it lives on the setting itself, so there
> is no separate screen to go and check.

> **Note:** A field reading **Using the default — nobody has set this** is not
> the same as a field somebody chose. The distinction matters when you are
> working out whether a value was decided or inherited.
