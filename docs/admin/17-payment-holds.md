## Who can do this

Operations staff and above. A hold stops somebody being paid, so it follows the
[[link:admin/permissions|permissions matrix]].

## Before you start

The caretaker's ID or the zone's, and a reason you are willing to have read back
to you.

## Steps

**1. Open Payment holds.**

![The payment holds page in the admin console, with the lifting note and the reason field marked. Marked on the screenshot: A hold never expires on its own. Required, and read by whoever is asked about it later.](/img/admin-payment-holds-01-en.png "Payment holds. What is held, from whom, and why.")

**2. Choose what the hold is on.**

A hold is on a **caretaker** or a **zone**, and it *"withholds everything for
this subject from every run, until somebody releases it"* — not just the next
run.

**3. Write the reason.**

The reason is required and at least four characters, and the form says who it is
for: *"Whoever is asked about this later."* Write it for that person, not for
yourself today.

## How you know it worked

The hold appears in the list as **Active**, and the next
[[link:admin/release-payout|compiled run]] shows the amount under **Held**.

## If it goes wrong

> **Warning:** *"Only a person can lift one."* A hold does not expire, lapse or
> clear itself when the underlying problem is fixed. Somebody has to come back
> and release it, and until they do the caretaker is not paid.

> **Note:** Holding a zone holds everybody in it. If the problem is one person,
> hold the person.
