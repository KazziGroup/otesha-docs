## Who can do this

Anybody on your organisation's account.

## Before you start

Nothing. If you are here to place an order rather than check one, see
[[link:corporate/bulk-order|Place a bulk order]].

## Steps

**1. Open Orders.**

![The corporate orders table, with the reference, the outstanding column and a completed order marked. Marked on the screenshot: The order reference, which its invoice quotes. Trees paid for that are not in the ground yet. Finished orders show a dash, not a zero.](/img/corporate-orders-01-en.png "Every order, with what is still owed on it.")

One row per order: its reference, how many trees, how many are still owed, the
total, its state, and the date it was placed.

**2. Read the Outstanding column.**

This is the number that matters. It is how many trees you have paid for that are
**not in the ground yet** — the order quantity less what has been planted. An
order for 40 trees with nothing planted shows *40*.

**3. Check the state.**

*Submitted* means placed and waiting to be planted. *Complete* means every tree
is in the ground.

## How you know it worked

Nothing to confirm; this is a record. A new order appears here immediately, as
**submitted**, with its full quantity outstanding.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** A completed order shows **—** in Outstanding, not **0**. The dash is
> meant to read as *done* rather than as a quantity, so do not go looking for a
> zero that is never printed.

> **Note:** If the table is replaced by an error rather than showing no rows,
> that is the difference between *we could not fetch your orders* and *you have
> no orders*. The page is built to never let the first one look like the second.
