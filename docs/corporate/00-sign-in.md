## Who can do this

Anybody on your organisation's account. Otesha staff set the account up and add
your number to it; you cannot create one by signing in.

## Before you start

The phone whose number is on the account, with signal.

## Steps

**1. Enter your phone number and tap Send code.**

![The corporate portal sign-in screen, with the phone field and the Send code button marked. Marked on the screenshot: The number on your corporate account. Sends a six-digit code by SMS.](/img/corporate-sign-in-01-en.png "The portal signs you in by SMS — there is no password field.")

**Corporate accounts have no password at all.** This is deliberate rather than
an omission: there is no shared secret to circulate when somebody joins or
leaves, and no password for anyone to paste into a browser they do not control.

**2. Enter the six digits.**

![The corporate code screen, with the code field and the sign-in button marked. Marked on the screenshot: The six digits from the SMS. Signs you into your organisation.](/img/corporate-sign-in-02-en.png "The code arrives by SMS, usually within a few seconds.")

## How you know it worked

You land on your organisation's **Dashboard** — trees, survival rate, land under
management. If you belong to more than one organisation you are asked which
first.

You see only your own organisation. Another organisation's data is not hidden
from you behind a permission; as far as this portal is concerned it does not
exist.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** The portal does not recognise your number. It has to be the number
> on the account, not a personal one — ask whoever set the account up, or email
> the support address on the sign-in screen.

> **Warning:** Trying to sign in with an email and password will always fail,
> even with credentials that work on the admin console. There is no password on
> a corporate account for anything to check.
