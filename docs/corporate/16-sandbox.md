## Who can do this

Anybody on an organisation that holds Otesha Connect.

## Before you start

A **sandbox** key. Live keys do not reach any of this.

## Steps

**1. Open Developers, then Sandbox.**

![The sandbox page in the developer console, with the reset description and the clock explanation marked. Marked on the screenshot: A known starting point, whenever an experiment has made a mess. Moving a tree's clock changes your test, not anybody else's data.](/img/corporate-sandbox-01-en.png "The sandbox: reset it, or move a tree forward in time.")

**2. Reset when you need a known starting point.**

**Reset sandbox** wipes yours and re-seeds it: *3 zones, 12 clusters, 8 species,
200 trees across all ten lifecycle states, 5 caretakers, 5 customers, and a year
of back-dated visits and photos.*

**3. Move a tree's clock to test what takes a year.**

Forward by 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, for one tree or every seeded one. That is
how you exercise milestones, health transitions and a billing cycle in an
afternoon.

## How you know it worked

The sandbox returns to the seeded shape, or the tree you advanced reports the
milestones it has now passed.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** It moves the **tree's** clock, not the server's. A shared clock
> would mean your test changed somebody else's data.

> **Note:** Reset touches nothing belonging to another partner and, by
> construction, nothing in live. It is safe whenever an experiment has made a
> mess.
