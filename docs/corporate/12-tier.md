## Who can do this

Anybody on your organisation's account.

## Before you start

Nothing. The tier is at the bottom of [[link:corporate/settings|Settings]].

## Steps

**1. Read your current tier.**

![The tier panel in the corporate portal, with the tier and its discount marked, and the recalculation note. Marked on the screenshot: Your tier, and what it takes off a new order. The discount is stamped on an order when it is placed.](/img/corporate-tier-01-en.png "Your tier: the discount it carries, and what the next one needs.")

**2. See what the next tier needs.**

Tiers are counted in **living trees**, not trees bought. Kilima Bank's 306
living trees put it on *sapling*; *grove* is 694 further on.

## How you know it worked

Nothing to confirm; this is a statement of where you stand.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** A discount is **stamped on an order when it is placed**. Reaching a
> new tier does not reprice anything you have already bought, and losing trees
> does not claw anything back.

> **Note:** The panel says when the tier was last recalculated. Between
> recalculations, the discount you see is the one a new order will get.

> **Note:** At *sapling* the discount is **0.0%**, so the estimate on the order
> form and the total on the invoice are the same figure. On a later tier they
> will not be.
