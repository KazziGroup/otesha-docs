## Who can do this

Anybody on your organisation's account.

## Before you start

An invoice has to exist. One is raised **when a bulk order is submitted**, not
when the trees are planted — so the invoice is usually the first thing an order
produces.

## Steps

**1. Open Invoices.**

![The corporate invoice list, with the unpaid total, the order reference and the PDF link marked. Marked on the screenshot: Totals cover the rows on screen, not the account. Which order the invoice is for. The invoice document, once it has been issued.](/img/corporate-invoices-01-en.png "Invoices: what is owed, what is late, and the document for each.")

**2. Read the three figures across the top.**

*On this page*, *Unpaid on this page*, *Overdue on this page*. The wording is
literal and worth taking seriously — see the note below.

**3. Find the invoice.**

The **For** column gives the order it belongs to, which is how you match an
invoice against [[link:corporate/order-history|your orders]]. **Issued** and
**Due** are thirty days apart.

**4. Download the document.**

**PDF** in the Document column. **Export CSV** takes the list itself, for
whoever does your reconciliation.

## How you know it worked

A paid invoice carries a date under **Paid** instead of a dash, and drops out of
the unpaid total.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** The totals say **on this page** because that is exactly what they
> are. The list pages at twenty-five rows, and the figures add up the rows in
> front of you — not your account. With more than twenty-five invoices, the
> unpaid total is not what you owe.

> **Note:** **Overdue** is worked out, not stored. An invoice shows the overdue
> badge when it is unpaid *and* past its due date; its own status is still
> *issued*. Nothing marks it overdue, so nothing has to run for it to appear.

> **Note:** Cancelling a bulk order voids its invoice at the same moment. An
> invoice already **paid** is not voided — getting that money back is a refund,
> which is a separate act, and the cancellation stands either way.
