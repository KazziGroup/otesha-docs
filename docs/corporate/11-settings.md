## Who can do this

Anybody on the account can change their own details. Billing details are an
owner's job.

## Before you start

Nothing.

## Steps

**1. Open Settings.**

![The corporate settings page, with the tax identification and the read-only explanation marked. Marked on the screenshot: How finance and an auditor identify the account. Read-only on purpose, with a record of who asked.](/img/corporate-settings-01-en.png "Settings: your details on the left, the organisation's on the right.")

**2. Edit what is yours.**

**Name**, **email** and **language** are yours to change. Email is optional —
set it and reports and receipts go there.

**3. Set the billing contact.**

**Billing email** is where invoices go, and it need not be the same address as
yours. Phone numbers are stored as `+255…` however you type them.

## How you know it worked

The page says *"Billing details updated."*

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** Your **phone number** is not editable here. It is how you sign in
> *and* how Otesha links you to every account you belong to, so changing it is a
> request rather than a field.

> **Note:** The organisation's **name** and **tax identification** are read-only
> for the same reason: *"they are how finance and an auditor identify this
> account"*. They change by asking us, with a record of who asked. An
> organisation cannot change its own **status** at all.
