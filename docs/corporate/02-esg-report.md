## Who can do this

Anybody on your organisation's account. You see only your own organisation —
Otesha staff cannot see it through this portal at all.

## Before you start

Decide the period. Every figure on the page counts only what was alive during
the dates you choose, so the range is not presentational — it changes the
numbers.

## Steps

**1. Open Reports and set the dates.**

![The Reports page in the corporate portal, with the report tabs and the Generate ESG report button marked. Marked on the screenshot: Produces the report for the dates above. The indicators the report will cover.](/img/corporate-esg-report-01-en.png "Reports · Carbon & ESG. The dates above govern every figure on the page.")

**2. Check which tabs have anything in them.**

**Carbon & ESG** is the headline. **SDG indicators** and **Certificates** carry
counts beside them, so a zero here means there is nothing to include for this
period rather than a page that failed to load.

**3. Generate the report.**

## How you know it worked

The report downloads, and it names what it excluded and whether a figure is
indicative — the CO₂ number is marked ±10% on the page for the same reason.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** Every figure reads zero. Check the dates first. A range in which no
> tree was yet in the ground is genuinely zero, and the page has no way to tell
> that apart from a range you did not mean to pick.
