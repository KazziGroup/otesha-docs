## Who can do this

Anybody on an organisation that holds Otesha Connect.

## Before you start

Nothing. It defaults to this calendar month.

## Steps

**1. Open Developers, then Usage.**

![The usage page in the developer console, with the new accounts and customers linked metrics marked. Marked on the screenshot: Accounts that exist because of your integration. Linked is not the same as new.](/img/corporate-usage-01-en.png "Usage: what came through your integration, and today's quota.")

**2. Set the period, or accept this month.**

The heading says which period and which environment the figures are for —
*1 Sept 2026 – 18 Sept 2026 · live*.

**3. Read the five figures.**

New accounts, customers linked, orders, trees, gross value.

## How you know it worked

Nothing to confirm; this is a report.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** **New accounts** and **customers linked** are different questions.
> Linked includes people who already had an Otesha account before they met your
> app; new counts only those your integration opened.

> **Note:** **Today's quota** belongs to the credential your integration
> authenticates with, and resets daily. Browsing this console does not spend it.

> **Note:** The figures are per environment. Sandbox traffic never appears in a
> live report.
