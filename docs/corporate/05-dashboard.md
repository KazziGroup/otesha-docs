## Who can do this

Anybody on your organisation's account.

## Before you start

Nothing. It is the screen you land on.

## Steps

**1. Sign in.**

![The corporate dashboard, with the tree count and the survival rate marked. Marked on the screenshot: Of trees ever in the ground, how many are alive. How many you have, and how many are living.](/img/corporate-dashboard-01-en.png "Your programme: trees, survival, and what they are doing.")

**2. Read the two headline figures together.**

**Trees** is how many you have and how many are living. **Survival** is the
share of trees *ever* in the ground that are still alive — so it counts trees
that have died, not trees that are struggling.

**3. Look at the breakdown by status.**

*Growing*, *planted*, *mature*, *at risk*, *dormant*. **At risk** trees are
alive and counted in survival; they are the ones a caretaker has flagged as
needing attention.

## How you know it worked

The figures match the [[link:corporate/tree-register|tree register]], which is the same
data listed rather than summarised.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** Survival can read 100% while trees are at risk. The two answer
> different questions: survival is *alive or not*, at risk is *how it is doing*.

> **Note:** Carbon is marked *±10% · indicative* here as everywhere else. It is
> an estimate from species and age, not a measurement.
