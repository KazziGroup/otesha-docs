## Who can do this

Anybody on your organisation's account.

## Before you start

Know the **species** and the **care plan** you want, and how many trees. If you
do not, [[link:corporate/tree-register|the register]] shows what you already
have and how it is doing.

## Steps

**1. Open Orders, then Order trees.**

![The bulk order form in the corporate portal, with the quantity field and the estimate marked. Marked on the screenshot: Trees, not money — the price follows from the plan. An estimate. The exact figure is confirmed when the order is placed.](/img/corporate-bulk-order-01-en.png "Ordering trees: a species, a care plan, and how many.")

**2. Choose a species, then a care plan.**

The plan is what Otesha actually does for the tree, and its price is per tree —
*African teak · 3-year care · 36 months · TZS 1,075,000*.

**3. Say how many trees.**

You order in **trees**, not money. The total follows.

**4. Place the order.**

## How you know it worked

The order appears in [[link:corporate/order-history|your orders]] as
**submitted**, with its full quantity outstanding, and
[[link:corporate/invoices|an invoice]] is raised at the same moment.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** The figure on the form is an **estimate** — *"About TZS 43,000,000
> before your tier discount. The exact figure is confirmed when the order is
> placed."* At [[link:corporate/tier|sapling]] the discount is 0%, so the two
> agree; on a later tier they will not.

> **Note:** Cancelling an order voids its invoice in the same breath, unless the
> invoice has already been paid — then it is a refund, which is a separate
> conversation.
