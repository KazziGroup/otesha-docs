## Who can do this

Anybody on an organisation that holds Otesha Connect.

## Before you start

Nothing. Statements are compiled by Otesha on your settlement cycle — monthly,
unless yours says otherwise.

## Steps

**1. Open Developers, then Settlements.**

![The settlements page in the developer console, with the commission column and the immutability note marked. Marked on the screenshot: Your commission on the period's orders. An issued statement is never edited; a correction is issued beside it.](/img/corporate-settlements-01-en.png "Settlements: gross, commission, net, and the document for each period.")

**2. Read the row for the period.**

Orders, gross, commission, net, its status, when it was issued and when it was
paid.

**3. Download the statement, or export the list as CSV.**

## How you know it worked

Nothing to confirm; this is a record.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** A statement is compiled from **the same basis as the usage report**,
> so the two agree. If they disagree, that is worth telling us about.

> **Note:** Once issued, a statement **cannot be edited**. If something is
> wrong, a correcting statement is issued alongside it — the original stays,
> because somebody has already filed it.
