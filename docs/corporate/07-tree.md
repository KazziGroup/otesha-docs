## Who can do this

Anybody on your organisation's account.

## Before you start

Find the tree in [[link:corporate/tree-register|the register]].

## Steps

**1. Open the tree.**

![A single tree in the corporate portal, with the caretaker and the photographs note marked. Marked on the screenshot: The caretaker who was there most recently. Photographs come from the logging step, not the office.](/img/corporate-tree-01-en.png "One tree: its state, its care level, who tends it, and what has happened to it.")

**2. Read who is looking after it.**

The card names the caretaker who **last tended this tree**. That is a real
person on a rota rather than an account manager: Otesha assigns every tree to
somebody who visits it.

**3. Read the history.**

Every state change with its date: *PLANTED 8 Sept 2025*, *AT_RISK 6 Oct 2025*.
This is the audit trail behind the number on your dashboard.

## How you know it worked

Nothing to confirm; this is a record.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** *"No photographs of this tree yet. Caretakers add them from the
> field as they tend it."* A photograph is optional when a caretaker records
> work, so a well-tended tree may have none. Their absence says nothing about
> the tree.

> **Note:** **Care level** is what was bought — *full* — and it decides what the
> caretaker is asked to do, not how often they choose to visit.
