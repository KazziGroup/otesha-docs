## Who can do this

Owners. Administrators and viewers can see the team but not change it — the
portal only shows the controls your role actually allows, so a button you can
see is a button that will work.

## Before you start

Your colleague's **phone number**. That is what they sign in with.

## Steps

**1. Open Team.**

![The corporate team page, with the phone hint, the role picker and your own row marked. Marked on the screenshot: A colleague is added by number, not by email. Three roles. Owners administer; everybody else reads. You cannot remove yourself, and the last owner cannot be removed at all.](/img/corporate-team-01-en.png "The team: who is on the account, and what each of them may do.")

**2. Enter their number, and a name if they are new to Otesha.**

**3. Choose a role.**

*Owner* administers the account — adding people, changing billing. *Administrator*
and *Viewer* read it. If they already use Otesha, the name is ignored; they keep
the one they have.

**4. Add to team.**

## How you know it worked

They appear in the table with their role and the date they were added. They can
sign in immediately, on their own number.

## If it goes wrong

> **Note:** Roles are **per organisation**, not per person. The same colleague
> can own one account and merely view another, and changing their role here
> changes nothing anywhere else.

> **Note:** Two removals are refused, and the table says so instead of offering
> a button. Your own row reads **You**, and a sole owner reads **Only owner** —
> locking every human out of an account is a support request, not a click.
